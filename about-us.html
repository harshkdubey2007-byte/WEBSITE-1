Stage 01 · Get found

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Being found by people who are already looking for what you sell. Slower than ads to start, and considerably cheaper once it is running.

What the work looks like

  • A technical audit first: speed, indexing, crawl errors, mobile
  • Keyword mapping and on-page work: titles, headings, internal links, schema
  • Google Business Profile set up properly, with posts, photos and review prompts

How you will know it is workingKeyword positions, clicks in Search Console, and calls or form fills from organic search.

See this service
Stage 02 · Get seen

Social Media Marketing

Turning up consistently, sounding like a person rather than a brand deck.

What the work looks like

  • A monthly calendar you approve before anything is published
  • Posting across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube
  • Replies to comments and DMs during working hours

How you will know it is workingEnquiries that came through a profile, a DM or a link in bio, tagged so they are counted, not guessed.

See this service
Stage 03 · Get clicks

Meta Ads and Google Ads

Google catches people already looking; Meta reaches people who were not. Each needs its own creative and its own expectations.

What the work looks like

  • Account structure built from scratch, or an audit of the one you have
  • Conversion tracking checked and proven before spend starts
  • Your ad accounts stay in your name, with us added as a partner

How you will know it is workingCost per result and the trend in it, including what we switched off and why.

See this service
Stage 04 · Earn trust

Content Writing

Words with a job to do, written in your voice, with the brief shown to you before anything is written.

What the work looks like

  • Service and location pages built around what people actually search for
  • Blog posts with a reason to exist, on a calendar you can see
  • Email and WhatsApp copy for offers and follow-ups

How you will know it is workingPages showing up for the terms they were written for, and people staying on them longer.

Stage 05 · Convert

Web Development

The site everything else points at. Quick on a phone, simple to edit, and built so a change to a headline does not need a developer.

What the work looks like

  • Static, dynamic and custom websites
  • E-commerce shops that take money reliably
  • Landing pages built for one offer and one action

How you will know it is workingConversion rate of each page against the traffic feeding it, and the cost per lead that results.

See this service
Stage 06 · Follow up

Lead Generation

Everything between a stranger noticing you and your phone ringing, so fewer people get lost in the gap.

What the work looks like

  • Landing pages and forms built for one offer at a time
  • Automated first replies so nobody waits overnight
  • Every source tagged, so you can see which channel produced which lead

How you will know it is workingCost per lead, and how many of those leads your team called qualified.
