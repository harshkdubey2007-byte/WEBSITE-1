eBuddha Digitech Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At eBuddha Digitech ("eBuddha Digitech", "we", "us", or "our"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us.

eBuddha Digitech operates as both a digital marketing training institute and a digital marketing service provider. We provide professional training courses to students and individuals and also provide digital marketing and related services to businesses, organizations, brands, and other clients.

By accessing or using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you:

  • Visit or use our website, www.ebuddha.in
  • Enquire about or enrol in our courses
  • Request a demo class, counselling session, or course information
  • Apply or register as a student
  • Purchase or use any course or training service
  • Enquire about or engage our digital marketing services
  • Communicate with our team
  • Submit information through our forms
  • Interact with us through email, phone, WhatsApp, social media, or other communication channels

eBuddha Digitech provides two primary categories of services:

A. Educational & Training Services

We provide digital marketing education and training programs, which may include courses, practical training, workshops, demo classes, mentorship, assignments, projects, certifications, and related educational activities.

B. Digital Marketing Services

We provide digital marketing and technology-related services to businesses and organizations, which may include the following:

  • Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
  • Social Media Marketing
  • Social Media Management
  • Meta Advertising
  • Google Advertising
  • Performance Marketing
  • Content Marketing
  • Content Creation
  • Website Development
  • Website Design
  • Branding
  • Online Advertising
  • Lead Generation
  • Digital Strategy
  • Other digital marketing and related services

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected in connection with both categories.
