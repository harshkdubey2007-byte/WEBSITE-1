What we do

Our Social Media Marketing Services

Social media management in six pieces of work. You can start with an audit and add the rest once you can see what each one is doing.

Organic Social Management

The everyday presence. A calendar you approve before anything goes out, reels and carousels shot and written here, stories on schedule, and a person answering the comments and DMs during working hours. Nothing is published that you have not seen, and no caption is recycled from another client.

  • Calendar approved a month ahead
  • Shooting, editing and captions
  • Comments and DMs answered
  • Profiles and highlights kept tidy

Paid Social on Meta Ads

Instagram and Facebook ads built as their own thing rather than a boost button. Audiences and creative go out a few at a time so we can tell which one did the work, and the cost per enquiry is read every week rather than explained at the end of the month.

  • Campaigns structured by intent
  • Creative tested a few at a time
  • Weekly cost-per-enquiry check
  • Your ad account, in your name

Reels and Video Production

A shoot day every month so the calendar never runs on stock footage. We plan the hooks first, shoot in a batch, and edit for sound off, because most of the feed is watched that way. Subtitles go on everything.

  • Monthly batch shoot day
  • Hooks planned before the shoot
  • Edited for watching without sound
  • Subtitles and covers included

Creator and Influencer Work

Usually local creators rather than big names, because a Bhopal audience trusts a face it recognizes. We shortlist, negotiate the rate, write the brief, and check the numbers afterwards against what was promised.

  • Shortlists with real engagement checked
  • Rates negotiated and written down
  • Briefs that leave room for their voice
  • Paid partnership labels applied properly

Community and Reviews

The unglamorous half. Questions answered the same day, complaints taken off a public thread and into a DM, and a steady habit of asking happy customers for a Google review instead of hoping one turns up.

  • Same-day replies during the week
  • Complaints handled, not deleted
  • Google and Facebook reviews chased
  • An escalation route agreed with you

Audit and Account Setup

A sensible place to start if you are not ready for a retainer. We go through what you already have, write down what is worth fixing in what order, and hand it over. You can act on it yourself, and plenty of people do.

  • Every profile reviewed and scored
  • Bios, highlights and pinned posts fixed
  • Tracking and pixels checked
  • A written plan you can act on alone

How it runs

Our Social Media Marketing Process

Six steps, in this order. The first one is not posting. It is finding out what an enquiry is worth to you.

  1. 01

    Audit and goals

    We review your profiles and your competitors, and agree which enquiries you want more of and what one customer is worth to you.

  2. 02

    Plan the calendar

    A monthly calendar built around your audience and your offers. You approve it before anything is published.

  3. 03

    Create

    Reels, photographs, carousels and captions made for a phone screen, in a monthly batch shoot so the calendar never runs on stock footage.

  4. 04

    Publish and engage

    Posts go out on schedule, and a person answers the comments and DMs during working hours.

  5. 05

    Boost and advertise

    The two or three posts a month that earn it get budget behind them, and Meta Ads run as their own campaigns, a few audiences at a time.

  6. 06

    Report and refine

    One report a month in plain language: what worked, what flopped and why we think so, and what changes next month.

Before you ask

Frequently Asked Questions About Social Media Marketing

How much do social media marketing services cost?

It depends on how many platforms you want managed, how much video you need and whether paid ads are included. Ad spend goes to Meta and Google directly and is separate from our fee. We quote after a first conversation rather than before, which is why there is no price list. If your budget is too small for what you are asking, we will say so and tell you what we would do in your place.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp Business. We match each platform to the job it is good at instead of posting the same thing everywhere.

Who approves what gets posted?

You do. A monthly calendar is approved before anything is published, and nothing goes out that you have not seen. No caption is recycled from another client.

Do you run Instagram and Facebook ads as well?

Yes. Paid social, mostly Meta Ads, is built as its own piece of work rather than a boost button. Audiences and creative go out a few at a time, the cost per enquiry is read every week, and the ad account stays in your name.

Can you create reels and videos for us?

Yes. We plan the hooks first, shoot in a monthly batch, and edit for sound off because most of the feed is watched that way. Subtitles and covers go on everything.

How do you measure social media results?

Reach and engagement matter, but the number we care about is enquiries that came through a profile, a DM or a link in bio. Those are tagged so they can be counted rather than guessed at. Saves, shares and watch time are the honest numbers for content; follower count tells you least.

How long before social media shows results?

Paid social can bring enquiries in the first week, though it usually takes a month of tuning before they are the right enquiries. Organic social builds over months of turning up consistently, and we review what is working every month.

Do you guarantee followers or viral posts?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What we commit to is the calendar, the quality of the work, replies to the people who write to you, and honest monthly reporting, including what flopped.

Do you handle comments, DMs and reviews?

Yes. Comments and DMs are answered during working hours, complaints are taken off a public thread and into a DM, and we chase Google and Facebook reviews from happy customers.

Do you work with businesses in other cities?

Yes. Our office is in MP Nagar, Bhopal, and we work with businesses across India and abroad. Planning, posting, ads and reporting all work remotely, and for video we agree how the footage is captured.
