Websites we build

Types of Websites We Design & Develop

The right kind of site depends on how often your content changes and what visitors need to do. We will tell you which one you need, even if it is the cheaper one.

Static websites

For sites whose content does not change every week. Fewer moving parts means faster pages, a smaller attack surface and cheaper hosting. A brochure site does not need a database.

You will know it works whenLoad time and Core Web Vitals are good on a mid-range Android over mobile data, and the hosting bill stays small.

Dynamic websites

For sites where content changes often and staff need to change it themselves: listings, courses, inventory, news. Admin screens are kept plain, with only the fields that are needed.

You will know it works whenYour team updates the site without calling us, and pages stay fast once there are a few hundred of them.

Custom websites

When the thing you need does not exist as a template: a booking flow with your rules in it, a calculator, a portal your customers log into. Built only when a ready-made option will not do, because custom costs more to keep alive.

You will know it works whenThe manual process it replaced is measured in hours a week. If we cannot name that process, the project usually should not start.

E-commerce websites

A shop that takes money reliably. Most of the difficulty is not the storefront; it is stock, delivery charges, returns and what happens when a payment half fails.

You will know it works whenCheckout completion is high and few orders need a human to fix them. The second one is the honest measure of a good build.

WordPress websites

A WordPress site your marketing team can edit without raising a ticket, built as a theme or a clean rebuild of what you have, with the plugins that are needed and no more.

You will know it works whenLoad time on a mid-range phone is good, and how often you need to call us to change something trends towards never.

Landing pages

One page, one offer, one action. Built to be pointed at by an ad and changed quickly when the ad changes. A campaign sent to a homepage is money left on the table.

You will know it works whenThe page converts against the campaign feeding it, and the cost per lead that results is read alongside the conversion rate.

How it runs

Our Website Development Process

Six steps, in this order. The first one is not design. It is finding out what the site has to do for your business.

  1. 01

    Discovery and scope

    We ask the boring questions: what a customer costs you today, what visitors need to do, and what you already have. You get a written plan you can argue with.

  2. 02

    Structure and content

    Pages and content are planned around how people search and how your team works, so each page has one job and the editing screens stay simple.

  3. 03

    Design

    Mobile-first layouts, shown in stages you can see and stop, with forms and copy matched to the offer.

  4. 04

    Build

    The site is built as static, WordPress, dynamic or custom, with speed work, SEO markup and tracking in from the start. Every change sits on a staging link first.

  5. 05

    Test and launch

    Tested on a mid-range phone over mobile data, with real form submissions and, for shops, real payment failures, before anything goes live.

  6. 06

    Handover and improve

    A recorded handover and training, source files and documentation, then a support window. We watch speed and conversion after launch, and tell you what to change.

Before you ask

Frequently Asked Questions About Website Development

How much does website design and development cost?

It depends on the type of site, the number of pages and the integrations it needs: a static brochure site, a WordPress site, an online shop and a custom portal are very different jobs. We quote after a first conversation rather than before, which is why there is no price list. If your budget is too small for what you are asking, we will say so and tell you what we would do in your place.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the type of site and how quickly content and approvals arrive. A static site is quicker than a shop or a custom build. The written plan at the start gives the stages and the dates, and designs are shown in stages you can see and stop.

Should I choose a static, dynamic or custom website?

Static if your content does not change every week, which means faster pages and cheaper hosting. Dynamic if your staff need to change content often, such as listings, courses, inventory or news. Custom only when the thing you need does not exist as a template, because custom costs more to keep alive.

Can my team edit the website without calling you?

That is the aim. WordPress and dynamic sites are set up so your team can add pages without breaking the design, with a recorded handover session for whoever joins later. How often you need to call us should trend towards never.

Do you build online shops?

Yes: catalog structure, variants and stock rules, payment gateway and COD flows tested with real failures, shipping rates, pin-code serviceability, returns, and GST-correct invoicing, with analytics and product feeds ready for the ad platforms on day one.

Can you fix a slow WordPress website?

Usually, yes. Most of the WordPress sites we inherit are slow because of what was bolted onto them, not because of WordPress. We handle images and caching, cut the plugins nobody uses and measure again on a mid-range phone over mobile data.

Will my website be ready for SEO?

Yes. Titles, headings, schema markup, sitemap and analytics are set up at build time rather than added later. For the ongoing work of ranking, see our SEO services.

Will I get the source files, and who owns the domain and hosting?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered to you, and for static sites the source is handed over so you are not tied to us to make changes. On custom builds the documentation is written for the next developer, not just for us.

What happens after the website launches?

There is a support window after launch, agreed up front, plus backups, updates and security hardening. For dynamic sites there is a staging copy and a rollback path. We also watch speed and conversion and tell you what to change.

Do you work with businesses in other cities?

Yes. Our office is in MP Nagar, Bhopal, and we work with businesses across India and abroad. Scoping, design reviews, builds and handover all work remotely.
