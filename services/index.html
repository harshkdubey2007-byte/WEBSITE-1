How we work

Four steps, and the first one is not design.

  1. 01

    We ask the boring questions

    What does one customer cost you today? Which enquiries do you turn away? Where did last year's budget actually go? We would rather spend the first week on this than guess in month three.

  2. 02

    You get a plan you can argue with

    Written down. What we will do, in what order, and what we expect each piece to move. If a line does not make sense for your business, say so on the call and it comes out.

  3. 03

    We build and ship in stages

    Ads go live a few at a time so we can tell what worked. Content follows a calendar you can see. Website changes sit on a staging link before they go anywhere near your customers.

  4. 04

    You get a report you can read

    Once a month, in plain language: what we spent, what came in, what changes next. Everything stays in your name, so the accounts are yours whether or not you stay with us.

Before you ask

The questions we get on the first call

How soon will I see results?

It depends which service. Paid ads can bring enquiries in the first week, though it usually takes a month of tuning before they are the right enquiries. SEO is slower and is better measured in quarters. Anyone promising you page one in thirty days is selling something.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes, and a lot of our work is with single-location businesses here in Bhopal. If the budget is too small for what you are asking, we will say so on the first call and tell you what we would do in your place instead.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope, and for ads it also depends on your monthly spend. We quote after the first conversation rather than before, which is why there is no price list on this page. A clinic and an e-commerce brand do not need the same thing.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Plenty of clients start with social media or a website and add the rest once they can see what it is doing. We would rather do one thing properly than four things thinly.

Who actually does the work?

Our own team, in our own office. Your account is not passed to a freelancer you have never spoken to.

Do you train teams as well?

Yes. Training is the other half of what eBuddha Digitech does. If you would rather build the skill inside your own team, the course is a better use of your money than a retainer.
