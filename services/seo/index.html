Why SEO

Why Your Business Needs SEO

Most customers search before they call, visit or buy. SEO is how your business shows up at that moment, without paying for every click.

  1. 01

    Customers start on Google

    People look up a service, a clinic or a course before they contact anyone. If you are not on the first page for those searches, a competitor is the one they find.

  2. 02

    “Near me” searches are local

    Searches like “SEO company in Bhopal” or “dentist near MP Nagar” are answered with map results and Google Business Profile listings. Local SEO decides who appears there.

  3. 03

    It keeps working after the spend stops

    Paid ads stop the day the budget stops. A page that ranks keeps bringing visitors, which is why SEO is slower to start and considerably cheaper once it is running.

  4. 04

    AI answers cite websites

    Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity draw on pages that are clear, well structured and trusted. The same SEO work that earns rankings helps you get cited.

How it runs

Our SEO Process

Six steps, in this order. SEO is measured in quarters, not days, and anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling something.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We ask what a customer is worth to you, which enquiries you want more of, and who you compete with. Without that, there is no way to tell good SEO from busy SEO.

  2. 02

    Audit

    A technical and competitor audit: crawl, indexing, speed, mobile, structured data, your Business Profile, and the pages that already rank for your market.

  3. 03

    Keyword and intent map

    Every page gets one main keyword and one clear intent, local terms for Bhopal and wider terms for the rest of India, so pages stop competing with each other.

  4. 04

    Fix and build

    Technical fixes come first, then on-page changes, new pages and content, shipped in stages. Changes sit on a staging link before they reach your visitors.

  5. 05

    Authority and local signals

    Business Profile upkeep, listings, reviews, earned links and answer-friendly structure, so both Google and AI search have reasons to trust you.

  6. 06

    Report and refine

    A monthly report and review call. If a term is not moving after a quarter, we say so and change the approach rather than quietly reporting a different metric.

Before you ask

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO Services

How much do SEO services cost?

It depends on the scope: the size of your site, how competitive your market is, and whether you need local, technical or content work. We quote after a first conversation rather than before, which is why there is no price list. If your budget is too small for what you are asking, we will say so and tell you what we would do in your place.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is slower than ads to start and is better measured in quarters. Technical fixes can show up within weeks, but ranking movement depends on competition. If a term is not moving after a quarter, we say so and change the approach. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling something.

What is the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO helps your pages rank for a topic wherever the searcher is. Local SEO helps you appear for searches tied to a place, such as “SEO company in Bhopal”, in the map results and your Google Business Profile. Most Bhopal businesses need both.

Can you get my business on the Google Maps results?

We improve the things Google looks at: a complete and accurate Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details, reviews, photos and location pages. Nobody controls the map results, so we cannot promise a position, but this is the work that moves you towards it.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google decides the rankings. What we commit to is the work in the plan, honest monthly reporting from your own Search Console and GA4, and telling you plainly when something is not working.

What is AI search optimization (GEO), and do I need it?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, is making your pages easy for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity to understand and cite: clear answers, FAQ sections with schema, and strong brand and author signals. It builds on good SEO, so we include it rather than sell it separately.

Do you work with businesses in other cities?

Yes. Our office is in MP Nagar, Bhopal, and we work with businesses across India and abroad. Reviews, calls and reports all work remotely.

What will I see in the monthly SEO report?

Positions for the keywords we mapped, impressions and clicks from Search Console, organic calls and form fills from GA4, what we changed and what it did, and the plan for next month, all in plain language.

Will I own my accounts and my content?

Yes. Search Console, Analytics, your Business Profile, the domain and the hosting are registered to you, and drafts and reports sit in a shared folder. If we part ways, the history stays with you.

Should I choose SEO or Google Ads first?

Ads can bring enquiries within the first week, and SEO builds over months. Most businesses use both: ads for the quick wins, SEO for steady enquiries. We will tell you which to start with after the first call. See our performance marketing service.
