Overview

What this programme covers

Affiliate marketing means recommending a product and earning a commission when someone buys through your link. This programme shows how to choose a niche you can stay with, select products worth recommending, create honest content and track what works.

You also learn the disclosure and platform rules that keep your account safe.

What you will learn

  • Choose a niche and audience
  • Find and compare affiliate programs and networks
  • Create review and comparison content
  • Bring in traffic from search, social and email
  • Track clicks and commissions and improve
Curriculum

Six modules, step by step

1

Affiliate Basics

How commissions, cookies, networks and payouts work.

2

Niche and Product Selection

Audience needs, product quality and programs worth joining.

3

Content That Converts

Reviews, comparisons and guides that help people decide.

4

Traffic Sources

SEO, social media, short video and email lists.

5

Tracking and Compliance

Link tracking, disclosures and platform rules.

6

Scaling Up

What to double down on and what to drop.

No. The focus is on organic content and traffic.
