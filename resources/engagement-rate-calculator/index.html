How is engagement rate calculated?

Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ followers × 100. For several posts, the tool uses the average per post. If you add reach, it also shows the rate by reach.

What is a good engagement rate on Instagram?

As a general guide, 1% to 3% is average, 3% to 6% is good and above 6% is excellent. Small accounts usually have higher rates than large ones.

Where do I find these numbers?

Open Insights on Instagram or Facebook, or Analytics on LinkedIn, for any post. Likes, comments, shares, saves and reach are all listed there.
