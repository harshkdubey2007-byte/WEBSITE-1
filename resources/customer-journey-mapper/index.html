Most businesses lose people at Consideration. Reviews, clear prices and fast replies fix most of it.

What is a customer journey map?

It is a simple picture of the steps a customer goes through, from first hearing about you to buying and recommending you, with what they think and feel at each step.

How do I use the map?

Check each stage and ask: are we doing the actions listed here? Fix the weakest stage first, then track the KPIs every month.

Can I use it for a college project or a client?

Yes. Download the PDF and add your own research, like real customer quotes and numbers, to make it stronger.

Yes. Our Digital Marketing course covers customer research, funnels, content and ads with live projects.
