This tool runs in your browser. Your numbers are not saved or sent anywhere.

Where do I find these numbers?

In Google Search Console, open Performance, then Search results. Set the date to last month and compare with the previous month. Clicks, impressions, CTR and position are at the top.

How do I save the report as PDF?

Press Download PDF. The print window opens: choose Save as PDF as the printer, then save.

Are my numbers saved?

No. Everything stays in your browser. Download the PDF or copy the summary before you close the page.
