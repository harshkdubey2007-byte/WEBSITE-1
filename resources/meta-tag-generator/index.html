Put your main keyword near the start of the title. Google and readers notice it faster.

How long should a meta title be?

Keep it between 30 and 60 characters. Longer titles are usually cut off in Google results.

How long should a meta description be?

Aim for 120 to 160 characters. Shorter is fine, but longer descriptions are usually cut off.

Where do I paste the code?

Paste it inside the head section of your page. In WordPress, use an SEO plugin and paste the title and description into its boxes instead.
