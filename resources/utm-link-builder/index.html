Tips for better results

Keep names consistent

Always write facebook, not Facebook or FB. Analytics counts each spelling as a different source.

Use for every campaign

Tag links in ads, bios, emails and WhatsApp messages so you can see which one brings visits and sales.

Do not tag internal links

Use UTM links only for links from outside your site. Tagging your own menu links breaks your reports.

This tool uses proven formulas, not paid AI. Your details stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

What is a UTM link?

A UTM link is your normal link with small tags added at the end. They tell Google Analytics where a visit came from, like a Facebook ad or an email.

Which fields do I need?

Website link, source, medium and campaign name are needed. Keyword and content are optional and help when you run many ads.

Where do I see UTM results?

In Google Analytics 4, go to Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition. Change the table to show session source, medium or campaign.
