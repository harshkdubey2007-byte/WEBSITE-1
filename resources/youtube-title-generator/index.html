Tips for better results

Keyword first

Put your main keyword at the start of the title. Keep titles under 70 characters so they are not cut off.

Use the first 2 lines

The first two lines of the description show in search. Say what the video is about and use the keyword.

Add chapters

Chapters (0:00 Intro and so on) help viewers jump ahead and can show in Google search.

This tool uses proven YouTube SEO formulas, not paid AI. Your details stay in your browser and are not saved.

How long should a YouTube title be?

YouTube allows 100 characters, but titles over about 70 characters get cut off in search. Keep the key words at the start.

Do YouTube tags still matter?

Tags help a little, mostly when your topic is often misspelled. Titles, descriptions and thumbnails matter much more. The tool keeps tags under the 500 character limit.

How do chapters work?

Add times in the description starting with 0:00. You need at least 3 chapters, each 10 seconds or longer. Change the sample times to match your video.
