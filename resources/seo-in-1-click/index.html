SEO in 1 Click

Add one button to your bookmarks bar. Then check the SEO of any website, yours or a competitor’s, with a single click. No install, no sign-up.

Add it in 10 seconds

Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Safari on computer.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bareBuddha SEOCan’t see the bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Open any website you want to check.
  3. Click “eBuddha SEO” in your bookmarks bar. Click it again (or press Esc) to close.
Can’t drag? Add it by hand
  1. Press Copy code below.
  2. Bookmark any page (Ctrl+D), then edit that bookmark.
  3. Name it eBuddha SEO, paste the code into the URL box and save.

Tips for better results

Check competitors

Open a competitor’s top page and click eBuddha SEO to see their title, headings and schema in seconds.

Fix red dots first

Red means missing or broken, like no description or a noindex tag. Amber means it could be better.

Use the Copy report

Copy the summary into your audit sheet or send it to your client or teacher.

The bookmarklet runs only in your own browser on the page you open. It does not send any data anywhere.

What is a bookmarklet?

A bookmarklet is a bookmark that runs a small tool instead of opening a page. Click it on any website and it shows an SEO panel on top of that page. Nothing to install.

Does it work on mobile?

It is made for computers, where the bookmarks bar is easy to use. On phones, bookmarklets are hard to run, so use it on a laptop or desktop.

Why does it not open on some websites?

A few websites block bookmarklets with strict security settings (for example some banking or code sites). It works on almost all normal business websites and blogs.

Can I learn SEO audits properly?

Yes. Our SEO course covers on-page and technical SEO audits with live projects. Also try our On-Page SEO Checker.
