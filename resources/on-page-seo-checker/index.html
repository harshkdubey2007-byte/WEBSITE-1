How do I get my page HTML?

Open your page in Chrome, press Ctrl+U (or right-click and choose View page source), select all with Ctrl+A, copy and paste it here.

Why can’t I just enter a URL?

Browsers do not allow one website to read another website’s code directly. Pasting the HTML keeps it simple, and nothing is uploaded.

Is a score of 100 needed to rank?

No. The score is a checklist of on-page basics. Rankings also depend on content quality, links and search intent. Aim to fix all red items.
