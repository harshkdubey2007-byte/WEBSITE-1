How to use your ad copy

Google Search ads

Add at least 3 and up to 15 headlines and 2 to 4 descriptions to a Responsive Search Ad. Google mixes them to find the best combination.

Meta ads

Use one primary text per ad and test 2 to 3 versions. Keep the first line strong: only about 125 characters show before “See more”.

Before you publish

Read every line, make sure each claim is true for your business, and change anything that does not sound like you.

This tool uses proven ad-writing formulas, not paid AI. Your details stay in your browser and are not saved.

Yes. It is completely free to use, with no sign-up. Generate as many versions as you like.

What are the character limits for Google Search ads?

Headlines can be up to 30 characters and descriptions up to 90 characters. The tool only shows lines that fit, with a live count on each.

How much text shows in a Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad?

About the first 125 characters of the primary text show before “See more”, and headlines work best under 40 characters. The tool keeps to these lengths.
