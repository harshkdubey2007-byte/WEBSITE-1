Tips for better results

Talk benefits, not problems

Say what people get (“Feel fitter in 30 days”), not what is wrong with them (“Are you overweight?”).

Keep claims honest

Use real numbers you can prove. Avoid “guaranteed”, “100%” and “overnight”.

Check images too

Avoid before-and-after photos, fake play buttons and shocking images. Keep text on images short.

Your ad text is checked inside your browser and is not saved or sent anywhere.

Will this guarantee my ad is approved?

No. It checks the text for common problems based on Meta’s Advertising Standards. Meta also reviews images, videos and your landing page, and makes the final decision.

What is a personal attribute in Meta ads?

Ads cannot assert or imply things about the viewer, like their health, weight, money, religion, ethnicity or relationship status. “Are you in debt?” is a common example.

What should I do if my ad is rejected?

Read the reason in Account Quality, fix the text, image or landing page, then edit the ad or request a review if you think it was a mistake.
