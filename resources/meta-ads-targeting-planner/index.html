Tips for better results

Broad often wins

With good creative, broad audiences and Advantage+ often beat narrow interest targeting. Always test broad.

Creative is the new targeting

Your hook, video and offer decide who engages. Test 3 to 5 different creatives in each ad set.

Retarget everyone warm

Video viewers, engagers and website visitors are your cheapest leads. Always keep a warm campaign running.

This tool uses proven Meta Ads structures, not paid data. Your details stay in your browser and are not saved.

Should I use interests or broad targeting?

Test both. Broad (Advantage+ audience) lets Meta’s AI find buyers and often works well with strong creative. Interest stacks help when your product is very niche or your budget is small.

What size should my audience be?

For a city, aim for at least a few lakh people. For India-wide campaigns, millions. Very small audiences get expensive quickly.

What is the learning phase?

After you start or edit an ad set, Meta needs about 50 results in a week to learn who converts. Too many small ad sets can stay stuck in learning.

Can I learn Meta Ads properly?

Yes. Our Performance Marketing course covers Meta Ads, audiences, Pixel, creatives and scaling with live projects.
