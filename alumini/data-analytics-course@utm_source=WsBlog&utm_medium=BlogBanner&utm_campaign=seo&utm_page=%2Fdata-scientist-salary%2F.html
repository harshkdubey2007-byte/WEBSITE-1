Excited to share my latest Power BI project focused on *Revenue Optimization for ITC Hotels!* 🏨📊

* ⚠ A significant *cancellation rate of 24.83%* is leading to a substantial *lost revenue of ₹199 million*. The increasing trend in cancellations week-over-week and month-over-month is particularly concerning.

* 🎉 Weekends show consistently high occupancy, presenting an opportunity to focus on strategies to boost weekday occupancy.
* 🔍 Noticed some potentially anomalous occupancy data for certain room categories (RT4, RT3, RT1, RT2), suggesting a need for data quality review.

This project underscored the power of data visualization in identifying key business challenges and potential solutions within the hospitality industry.
