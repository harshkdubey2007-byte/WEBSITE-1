From scattered blogs to a strategic content ecosystem — here's what I built for Nivaay Travels! Recently, I worked on a Content Cluster Strategy for a travel blog focused on Lakshadweep. The idea was simple: create one powerful pillar post and surround it with multiple focused cluster articles — all interconnected to drive both SEO performance and reader engagement. Here’s what the structure looks like: Pillar Article: Lakshadweep Tourism: A Complete Guide Clusters: Top islands, best time to visit, food & culture, travel hacks, budget stays, and water adventures Smart Linking: Each blog connects back to the pillar, forming a strong internal link network SEO Optimization: Target keywords, meta titles, descriptions, and snippet-friendly FAQs Content Calendar: A full April plan — structured, timely, and goal-driven This strategy doesn't just improve rankings — it builds authority.
