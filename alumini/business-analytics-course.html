Milestone 1 - Duration: 4 weeks

Learn how to think strategically about products by understanding systems, outcomes, and business goals. Discover user needs through structured research and transform insights into well-defined product requirements. Use product metrics, analytics, and experimentation to make data-driven decisions and build successful products.

Week 1

Systems Thinking and Outcome Mapping for Product Strategy

  • Introduction to Systems Thinking
  • System Structure and Organizational Systems
  • Feedback Loops and System Dynamics
  • Systems Thinking in Digital Products
  • Aligning Business Goals with Product Outcomes
  • Product Metrics and Outcome Measurement
  • Outcome Mapping and Opportunity Prioritization
Week 2

Opportunity Discovery Through User Research

  • Opportunity Discovery and Opportunity Solution Trees
  • Opportunity vs Feature Thinking
  • Problem Definition and Opportunity Sizing
  • Introduction to User Research
  • User Research Types and Methods
  • Conducting User Research (Interviews, Surveys, Observation)
  • Research Synthesis, Bias, and Ethics
Week 3

Problem Framing and Requirements Definition

  • Introduction to Problem Framing
  • Problem Statements and Root Cause Analysis
  • Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) and Problem Validation
  • Types of Product Requirements
  • Product Requirement Documentation and User Stories
  • Story Mapping and Requirement Structuring
  • User Story Quality, Prioritization, and Stakeholder Alignment
Week 4

Product Metrics, Analytics, and Data-Driven Decision Making

  • Introduction to Product Metrics and Measurement
  • Types of Product Metrics and the AARRR Framework
  • Vanity vs Actionable Metrics
  • Product Analytics Fundamentals
  • Funnel, Retention, and Cohort Analysis
  • Feature Adoption and Experimentation (A/B Testing)
  • Hypothesis-Driven Analysis and Data-Informed Decision Making

Strategic Product Analysis at Urban Company

You are working as a Business Analyst at Urban Company, a leading home services platform operating across India. The management has noticed that despite strong app downloads and growing service listings, repeat bookings have been declining steadily over the past two quarters and customer acquisition costs are rising.You have been asked to deliver a full strategic analysis by mapping the system behind booking behavior, connecting business goals to measurable product outcomes, identifying and researching the right opportunity areas, framing the core problem with precision, defining clear requirements, and building a metrics framework to track progress.

Connecting Business Outcomes at Swiggy

You are working at Swiggy, a leading food delivery platform. The management has asked you to connect their revenue growth goal to the right product outcomes and evaluate which proposed features actually move the needle.

Opportunity Discovery at IKEA

You are working at IKEA, a global furniture and home goods retailer. The management has asked you to investigate the decline in repeat purchases among urban customers and identify the real opportunity spaces driving this behavior.

Requirements Workshop at PhonePe

You are working at PhonePe, a leading digital payments platform. The management has asked you to lead a requirements workshop for a new bill-splitting feature and bring alignment across product, engineering, design, and compliance teams.

Data-Driven Decisions at Meesho

You are working at Meesho, a fast-growing social commerce platform. The management has asked you to investigate a 15% drop in repeat orders in Tier-2 cities and recommend which hypothesis to pursue first using a structured, data-driven approach.
