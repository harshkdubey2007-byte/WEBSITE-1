            When we talk about SEO, only a few things can be done on-site to optimize it and boost its search engine rankings. Marketers need to work on several off-page elements, too, for the success of the overall SEO strategy. 

            Off-page SEO is vital to digital marketing, as it helps enhance brand visibility and credibility. Brand mentions, guest posting, reviews, content marketing, social media, etc, help businesses build trust and provide long-term benefits. 

            In this article, we will explore different off-page SEO methods that you can use to build your brand’s strong online presence and drive organic traffic. 

            What Are Off-page SEO Techniques?

            Off-page SEO focuses on optimizing elements that are not on a website to improve its search engine rankings. By using off-page techniques, brands can get more online visibility in SERPs and drive organic traffic to their websites. 

            In other words, off-page is more about getting backlinks from third-party sites. It can be through various methods, such as social media, guest posting, reviews, podcasts, influencer marketing, etc. You can learn more about off-page SEO techniques and trends by enrolling in our SEO course online, which covers the basics and advanced topics of search engine optimization. 

            We must consider three key factors while working on SEO off-page techniques:

            Authority- The website from which you are trying to acquire backlinks must be authoritative. 

            Relevancy- It must be relevant to your niche.

            Reputation- It must have the potential to drive traffic to your website.

            Top 17 Off-page SEO Techniques in 2026 

            Here are the top 17 off-page techniques in SEO that marketers vouch for:

            1. Guest Blogging

            Guest posting refers to writing quality, optimized articles for a third-party site that provide valuable information to readers. It is one of the best off-page SEO and link-building strategies for building brand awareness.

            Guest blogging helps businesses attract attention from a wider audience by placing them in front of them. It drives traffic and establishes the guest author as a subject expert. The ultimate aim of guest blogging is to acquire new customers and gain exposure. 

            However, you need to take care of a few things. The guest blogging site must be relevant to your niche and industry to build credibility and reputation, ensure quality links, and improve brand visibility. 

            Also, Google doesn’t support paid guest posts or other manipulative strategies. So, avoid spammy methods and prefer only high-authority and reputable sites relevant to your business. 

            2. Social Media Marketing

            Over 90% of internet users are on social media channels. The ever-rising popularity of social media has paved the way for marketers and businesses to promote their products effectively to a larger audience and interact with people directly. Businesses can connect with the target audience, engage them with the brand, and create brand awareness. 

            Although social media is not a key search engine ranking factor, Google and other search engines use it to assess trustworthiness and credibility. Brands rely on various social media platforms to attract new users and encourage them to share, like, and comment on their posts.

            If done strategically, it can accelerate business growth. Companies consider it among the top off-page SEO techniques as it helps them build a personal brand and connect with relevant influencers. 

            3. Local SEO

            If you have a brick-and-mortar store, you would need local SEO to attract customers in the nearby area. It will help your business gain limelight within the local area and strengthen your online and offline presence. 

            Do you know that around 46% of Google searches are about finding local businesses? Also, 80% of searchers use Google Maps to find nearby stores or businesses. So, create your Google My Business profile, optimize your listing, and work on local SEO. 

            When people find you easily in local searches, they will also be eager to visit your store. Local SEO builds trust among the audience and increases visibility. 

            4. Influencer Marketing

            Big brands and companies collaborate with influencers to acquire links and build brand awareness. 1 out of every 4 marketers leverages the power of influencer marketing to connect with the target audience, especially Gen Z and Millenials. 

            So, if your potential customers belong to this age group, then influencer marketing is the best way to go. It is the most sought-after off-page SEO techniques 2026 that allows businesses to earn quality backlinks, social shares, and solid online presence. 

            You can find and reach out to influencers via social media. Make sure that the influencers you collaborate with are relevant to your industry and have a considerable following. 

            Also Read: Types of Backlinks in SEO (15 Different Off-Page Backlinks Types)

            5. Link Building

            There are almost 100 billion searches every month on Google related to products, services, brands, and content. Google and search engines are constantly trying to evaluate why and how people interact with specific web pages. Hence, Google considers over 200 factors to rank web pages in SERPs, and link-building is one of the primary considerations. 

            Backlinks are crucial for off-page SEO as they enable search engines to determine how valuable your content is. When you acquire links from reputed and credible websites, it means they trust your content and endorse it, boosting your search engine rankings. 

            The more authority links you get, the better your search engine rankings will be. Link building also includes finding broken links and replacing them with your link. Links must be do-follow, as they enable web crawlers to follow the given link and find your page.

            6. Brand Mentions

            Brand mentions are also link-building SEO off-page activities. It is a preferred SEO tactic for blogs that have been around for some time. Many people mention brand names without giving a proper link. 

            So, find unlinked mentions of your brand using a professional SEO tool to get more specific results. Prepare a list of all sites that have mentioned your brand. Send customized emails requesting to add your link with the brand name. 

            As they have already mentioned your brand name, they should be fine with adding the link. 

            7. Forum Submission

            Answering questions on forums is a great way to build quality links while connecting with different communities. You can discuss trending topics or common questions related to your industry. 

            Forums are also a trusted platform for resolving queries from your consumers and target audiences while displaying your industry expertise. Reply to threads, offer suggestions, answer queries, and share your opinion with peers to show your command over a topic. 

            Add do-follow links to drive traffic and build trust. This may be an outdated technique, but some companies still try it to earn a few relevant links. 

            8. Podcasts

            The popularity of podcasts is growing by leaps and bounds, and search engines often show podcast results on SERPs. Therefore, many marketers use it as a part of their content strategies. 

            Create podcasts to share your ideas, thoughts, and views on multiple topics related to one niche or industry. This will allow you to reach the right audience and establish a brand authority. You can also earn backlinks if they find your podcast useful and valuable. 

            It is also a chance to engage with users through interesting discussions and encourage them to share your videos with others. 

            Ensure that your podcast's metadata, show name, and description are optimized to make it searchable and enhance its search engine visibility. 

            9. Press Releases

            Next on our list of off-page SEO techniques is press releases, which are news articles released by the PR department of a company and distributed across different media channels. 

            It is a trusted link-building and marketing strategy to earn high-authority links and maintain an excellent brand reputation. It requires you to up your PR game to promote narratives and earn ample links. 

            Press releases increase brand searches and tell people about a specific product, service, upcoming events, latest deals, and product launch. It gets your business much-needed attention from potential customers and creates a buzz around your brand. You can establish yourself as a thought leader in your field and share your message on a bigger scale.

            10. Image and Video Submission

            Share images related to your brand, products, and services on various image submission sites to attract people and drive traffic to your website. However, optimize pictures using the correct URL and meta tags before you submit them. Images should have relevant and SEO-friendly descriptions, titles, and tags.

            Video submission is also similar. It can be a short video like Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts or a long informative video, spanning 10-15 minutes. Connect with top video submission sites and submit videos with proper descriptions, tags, and reference links. 

            11. Document Sharing

            Some highly-competitive keywords are difficult to rank for. However, document sharing is your chance to rank for those keywords and compete with bigger brands. You can submit your documents on different sites and boost your off-page SEO efforts.

            If you think your content is beneficial for users, submit it on a document-sharing platform. Make sure you write it after in-depth research. Optimize different SEO elements, including transcripts, titles, and file names. You can also add CTAs and links wherever appropriate. 

            Search engines may not index PDFs and PPTs, but they can read these files, which can help you achieve higher rankings. 

            Essential SEO Topics You Should Know

            12. Question and Answer 

            Most of you must know about Quora and being a marketer or a business owner; if you are still not using it, you are missing out on ample opportunities. It is one of the best off-page SEO techniques where you answer common questions related to your industry or expertise and connect with people. 

            Whether you want to earn quality links, drive traffic, or enhance your brand’s presence, Quora and other Q&A sites are one-stop solutions. What’s more? They are easy to use. Just create a profile, find questions, and start answering. 

            Your answers should be fact-checked and helpful, showing your expertise in your field. Using Quora is a long-term strategy to bring traffic, as Google often shows Quora answers in SERPs. You can also uncover different sales opportunities and generate leads. 

            13. Infographics

            People are done with boring images and lengthy texts. They no longer want to read keyword-stuffed articles. Moreover, efforts to build more links often result in spam. So, it’s critical to come up with something that stands out and gets Google’s recognition. 

            When you submit or post unique and innovative images, they tend to capture attention and links from top sites, which can boost your off-page SEO. Hence, businesses use infographics, which generate over 37% more backlinks than other posts. They are among the most liked and shared content as the human brain processes visuals and images 60,000 times faster than plain texts. 

            Create attractive designs with vibrant colors and informative icons, charts, and graphs that share important data with users. This can help you earn additional links from reputed sites and contribute to your business success. 

            14. Blog Commenting

            Select a few top blogs related to your field, add useful comments or opinions, and people will start noticing you. Your comments should pique the interest of readers so they are compelled to check your website.

            If you have informative and valuable content on your site, visitors will stay longer and rely on you for reliable information whenever needed. This will send positive signals to search engines and boost your rankings on SERPs. 

            Moreover, blog comments get you links and build a strong backlink profile. It can work wonders for a new blogger. 

            Your comments must be genuine and make sense to readers. Don’t leave too many comments else, it would look spammy. 

            15. Social Bookmarking

            Social bookmarking sites promote websites and enhance off-page SEO. Pick a few social bookmarking sites for your blogs and web pages, add links to those sites, and be consistent with the process.

            Don’t overdo it. Stick to popular and trusted sites to gain quality and relevant traffic to your pages.

            16. Local Citation

            Create your business profile on local directories and citation sites like Yelp, Capterra, and G2 to get backlinks. It is mainly used by local businesses to spread the word about their products or services and bring people to their physical stores. 

            It’s important to update your profile regularly and keep business information, such as name, address, and contact details, consistent. If the information is inconsistent, Google may not index your site. 

            Also, try adding images, videos, reviews, and other required details about your business.

            17. Creating Shareable Content

            Last on our off-page SEO techniques list is creating highly optimized content. This is the best method for marketers who don’t want natural backlinks without implementing any link-building campaign. 

            Start by finding quality content that you can use to create a well-researched article. Build internal links and do SEO to enhance its search engine ranking. Add data-driven keywords and you’ll see backlinks pouring in naturally. 

            Reports, stats posts, case studies, surveys, data posts, industry insights, unique research, and interviews are different types of write-ups that you can create to earn links.

            Writers often seek data and facts to support their content and make it more credible. By ensuring the content is original and insightful, you can attract ample natural links to these posts.

            Off-page SEO techniques are crucial for any SEO campaign, improving a website’s authority, visibility, and organic rankings. We hope you enjoyed this blog and that it helped you brush up on your knowledge of the latest SEO off-page activities and trends.
