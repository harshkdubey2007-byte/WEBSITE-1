About WsCube Tech

WsCube Tech is an upskilling edtech startup dedicated to building careers in various tech/non-tech domains digital fields, including Data Analytics, Digital Marketing, Web Development, Cybersecurity and more. We offer a range of structured cohort-based and flexiblecourses mentorship programs designed to equip you with the skills needed to excel in professional career sphere the digital world.

  • Industry-Relevant Job Ready Curriculum: Courses are designed by experts and cover the latest tools, techniques, and trends in the industry.

  • Mentor-led Live Masterclasses  Live Interactive Classes: Engage with instructors in real-time during live sessions that fit into your schedule.

  • Project-based Upskilling Practical Learning Approach: Gain hands-on experience with projects and assignments that mimic real-world scenarios.

  • Doubt Resolution Support: Access a dedicated team of teaching assistants to help you with any questions or challenges.

  • End to End Career Support Guidance: Benefit from WsCube Tech’s 7 Days Last-mile Prep Module with career support services, including resume building, mock interviews, and job placement assistance.

Flexible Learning: Access recorded lectures and switch between batches at no extra cost, making it easy to learn at your own pace. Subject to Cohort Regulations.
