WHERE WE WORK

Industries We Serve

eBuddha Digitech runs digital marketing for small and mid-sized businesses across India, from our centre in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. These are the sectors our team works in most often, and what the work usually looks like in each.

  1. Schools, coaching institutes and training brands that need a steady flow of admission enquiries rather than one-off campaigns.

    Our recent clients

  2. Clinics, diagnostics and wellness practices, where most of the work is local search, Maps visibility and appointment enquiries.

    Our recent clients

  3. Builders, brokers and interior studios running project launches on paid search and social, measured on site visits booked.

    Our recent clients

  4. Product brands selling online and in store - catalogue, creative and retargeting, priced against return on ad spend.

    Our recent clients

  5. Restaurants, cafes and hotels, where reviews, Maps and social content decide who walks in this weekend.

    Our recent clients

  6. Chartered accountants, lawyers and consultants who need authority content and a lead pipeline that does not depend on referrals.
