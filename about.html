WHERE WE WORK

Industries We Serve

eBuddha Digitech runs digital marketing for small and mid-sized businesses across India, from our centre in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. These are the sectors our team works in most often, and what the work usually looks like in each.

Education & Coaching

Schools, coaching institutes and training brands that need a steady flow of admission enquiries rather than one-off campaigns.

Healthcare & Wellness

Clinics, diagnostics and wellness practices, where most of the work is local search, Maps visibility and appointment enquiries.

Real Estate & Construction

Builders, brokers and interior studios running project launches on paid search and social, measured on site visits booked.

Retail & D2C Brands

Product brands selling online and in store - catalogue, creative and retargeting, priced against return on ad spend.

Hospitality & Food Service

Restaurants, cafes and hotels, where reviews, Maps and social content decide who walks in this weekend.

Professional Services

Chartered accountants, lawyers and consultants who need authority content and a lead pipeline that does not depend on referrals.

Manufacturing & B2B

Long-cycle businesses selling to other businesses, where search intent and a credible site do most of the qualifying.

Non-Profit & Community

Foundations and community organisations raising awareness and support on small, carefully spent budgets.

IT & Technology

Software and IT services firms competing for a small pool of high-intent searches every month.
