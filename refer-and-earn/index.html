Refer & Earn

You already tell people about us. Now it pays to.

Send a friend, a cousin, a colleague or a junior to any eBuddha Digitech programme. If they enrol, you earn a reward. One message with their name and number is the whole of it.

Refer someone now See how it works

Open to students, alumni, parents and anyone else who rates the training.

The short version

  • Anyone can refer
  • Any paid programme
  • A reward for each enrolment
  • Paid once their programme starts

What you get

A reward per enrolment, and no small print you have to hunt for.

The amount is not the same for every programme, so rather than print a number here that goes stale, we tell you what your referral is worth at the point they enrol. Everything else about the arrangement is on this page.

01

A reward for every enrolment

Each person who joins on your referral earns you a reward. Longer programmes carry a bigger one, so the amount depends on what they sign up for. We tell you the figure for their programme before they pay, not after.

02

Refer as many as you like

There is no cap. Each enrolment is counted on its own, whether it is the first person you send us or the fifteenth.

03

Nothing to sign up for

No portal, no code to remember, no form to keep hold of. Send us a name and a number and the record sits on our side.

How it works

Four steps, and only the first one is yours.

  1. 01

    Send us their name and number

    A message on WhatsApp is enough. If you would rather, ask them to give your name when they enquire. Either way, get it to us before they enrol, because that is the point at which the referral is tagged.

  2. 02

    We speak to them

    A counsellor calls, answers what they want to know and tells them honestly which programme fits. No pressure and no chasing, so nothing that would make you regret passing on their number.

  3. 03

    They join a programme

    When they enrol we confirm the reward for that programme with you, so you know the amount before any money changes hands.

  4. 04

    Your reward comes through

    Once their programme has started we pay it out to the account you give us. You hear from us at each step rather than having to ask.

What counts

Every paid programme we run.

Point them at whichever one fits. If they are not sure, send them anyway and the counsellor will work it out with them on the call.

Before you ask

The questions people send us about this

Who can refer someone?

Anyone. You do not have to be a student or an alumnus of ours. Parents, colleagues, friends and old batchmates send us people all the time, and the reward works the same way for all of them.

How do you know the referral came from me?

Because you told us first. Send the name and number across before they enrol, or have them give your name when they enquire. Whichever reaches us first is what goes on the record.

What if two people refer the same person?

The first name on our record is the one the reward goes to. That is the whole reason to send the details in early rather than after the conversation has already happened.

When is the reward paid?

After their programme has started. We confirm the amount at the time they enrol and pay it to the account you give us.

Which programmes count?

Every paid eBuddha Digitech programme. The free courses and the masterclasses do not carry a reward, though they are a good way to introduce someone before they commit.

Can I refer myself?

No. The reward is for bringing somebody else to us. If you are looking at a programme for yourself, ask us about the fee directly and we will tell you what is available.

Is there paperwork?

Only the account details we need in order to pay you. There is no agreement to sign and nothing that ties you to us.
