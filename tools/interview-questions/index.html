Tips for better results

Say it out loud

Read the question, close your eyes and answer out loud before you look. That is how real interviews feel.

Use the STAR method

For project questions, explain the Situation, Task, Action and Result, with numbers.

Practise a little daily

Ten questions a day for two weeks beats cramming the night before.
