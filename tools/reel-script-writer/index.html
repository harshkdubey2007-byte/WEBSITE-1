What is a hook in a Reel?

The hook is the first 1 to 3 seconds: the first line you say and the text on screen. It decides if people stop scrolling and keep watching.

What should I write in the [highlighted] parts?

They are the parts only you know, like your real tip, product, offer or result. Replace them with short, simple words before you film.
