Tips for better results

One page is enough

Freshers should keep the resume to one page. Show your best 2 to 4 projects with numbers.

Numbers make you stand out

“Grew reach by 40% in 2 months” is much stronger than “handled Instagram”. Use real numbers only.

Match each job

Read the job post and tick the skills it asks for that you really have. ATS software looks for those words.
