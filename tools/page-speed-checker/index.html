Tips for better results

Mobile matters most

Google ranks pages mainly using the mobile version. Fix the mobile score first.

Images are the usual problem

Big images are the most common reason for slow pages. Compress them and use WebP.

Test key pages

Check your home page, top service pages and top blog posts, not only the home page.

Your link is sent to Google PageSpeed Insights to run the test. Nothing is saved by us.

What is a good speed score?

90 to 100 is good, 50 to 89 needs work and under 50 is poor. Scores change a little each time you test, so compare a few runs.

What are Core Web Vitals?

They are three Google measures of user experience: LCP (how fast the main content shows), INP (how fast the page reacts to taps) and CLS (how much the page jumps).

Why is my mobile score lower than desktop?

The mobile test uses a mid-range phone on a slow 4G connection, so heavy images and scripts hurt much more.
