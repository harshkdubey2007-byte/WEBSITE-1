Tips for better posts

Hook in the first line

Only the first line or two show before “more”. Start with a question, a number or a bold promise.

Mix your hashtags

Use a few popular tags, more niche tags and one or two local or brand tags. Relevant beats many.

End with one clear action

Ask people to comment, save, share, visit or DM you. One action works better than three.
